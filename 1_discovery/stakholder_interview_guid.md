# Technician Interview Script: Uncovering Hidden Operational Costs

**Purpose:** Extract tacit knowledge about project execution, unplanned work, and hidden costs that are not captured in current forms.

**Duration:** 60 minutes per technician.
**Participants:** 1 Interviewer + 1 Technician (no supervisors/managers present).

---

## Pre-Interview Setup

1. **Select 4 technicians:** 
   - 2 Senior (10+ years)
   - 2 Junior (1-3 years)
2. **Select 3-5 projects** per technician that they worked on recently (mix of CCTV, Shutters, Alarms, Repairs).
3. **Bring printed copies** of their timesheets for those projects.

---

## Interview Script 

### Section A: The "Standard" vs. "Reality" Gap

*"Let's look at Project [X] on your timesheet. The quote said it should take 4 hours. How long did it actually take you, and why?"*

**Probes:**
- Did you have to wait for the client to arrive with keys? How long?
- Did you have to go back to the van for a tool you forgot?
- Did you have to re-do any work because something was installed incorrectly?

---

### Section B: The "Unplanned Work" Trap

*"On that same project, was there any work that you had to do that wasn't in the original scope?"*

**Probes:**
- Did you have to demolish part of a wall? (P-DEMO)
- Did you have to weld something? (P-WELD)
- Did you have to remove old equipment before installing the new one? (P-REMOVE)
- Did you have to do concealed wiring instead of surface wiring? (P-REWIRE)

---

### Section C: The "Tool Shuttle" Problem

*"Did you have to borrow a tool from another site or go back to the warehouse mid-project to get something?"*

**Probes:**
- How much extra driving did that add? (KM)
- Did you have to wait for that tool to become available?

---

### Section D: The "Warranty Return" Leak

*"For repair projects, how many visits did it take to fix the problem completely?"*

**Probes:**
- If it took more than 1 visit, why?
- Did you have to bring the part back to the workshop to repair it? (Bench repair)
- Was this a warranty call (free for the client)?

---

### Section E: The "Client Behavior" Wildcard

*"Did the client ask for changes after you finished the work?"*

**Probes:**
- Did they ask you to move a camera after it was installed? (P-RELOCATE)
- Did they ask for extra equipment that wasn't quoted? (Add-on)

---

### Closing Question (The Goldmine)

*"If I gave you a magic wand to redo this project perfectly, with zero budget constraints, how much faster could you do it, and what would you change?"*

---

## Post-Interview Action

1. **Transcribe key quotes** (keep them anonymous).
2. **Map identified issues** to our plugin library (P-DEMO, P-WELD, etc.).
3. **Bring insights** to the CEO meeting as evidence.
